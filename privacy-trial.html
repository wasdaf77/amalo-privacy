Privacy policy for Amalo watch faces with a trial

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

It covers the Amalo watch faces with a free trial and a paid unlock (Amalo Skyward Trial and the other "Trial" faces).

Summary

The data the watch face shows (activity, health, weather, location…) is processed only on your watch and is not sent to anyone. For the trial and the unlock, the watch sends WearCheckout an identifier of the watch and a few technical details. There is no advertising or analytics.

Data used on your watch

It is the same as in the paid version of each watch face and is processed only on the watch. It is described in the Meridian, Atelier, Tempo, Skyward and Bridge policy and in the Amalo Abroad policy.

Data for the trial and the unlock

From its background process, the watch face asks WearCheckout whether the trial is still running or the watch is already unlocked. Each request sends:

  • the watch's unique identifier assigned by Garmin;
  • the watch's language;
  • the watch model code;
  • the version of the payment library.

WearCheckout also stores the country the first request came from (two letters, without the IP address), the trial dates and, if you buy, the purchase status, amount, currency and payment identifiers.

Payment

Payment is processed by PayPal or Stripe, which collect your payment details (for example, your email and your card or PayPal account) under their own policies. We never see your card details. We receive the purchase details the processor gives us, such as your email and country, to handle refunds, customer support and tax obligations.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Trial and unlock: to provide what you sign up for (the 1-day trial and the paid unlock), and so that reinstalling the watch face does not restart the trial.
  • Tax and accounting: to comply with the law, which requires keeping sales records, including the buyer's country, for the period it sets.

Who processes the data

The controller is the seller of the watch faces (see "Contact"). WearCheckout provides the payment and unlock service on our behalf, and PayPal and Stripe process payments, each under its own policy: WearCheckout, PayPal and Stripe. Downloads and the Connect IQ app are handled by Garmin, under its own policy.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing, or ask for its restriction or portability, by writing to contact@amalo.es. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to the authority where you live.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the watch face deletes its settings and state from the watch. WearCheckout and sales data are deleted on request, except what the law requires us to keep.

Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published here with its new date.
