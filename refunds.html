Refund policy for Amalo watch faces with a trial

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

What it covers

Unlocks of Amalo watch faces with a free trial (Amalo Skyward Trial and the other "Trial" faces) paid outside the Connect IQ Store, on the payment page that the QR code or the watch's notice opens. Purchases made in the Connect IQ Store follow Garmin's policy.

Try before you pay

Each watch face can be used in full, for free, for 1 day before you pay. When the trial ends, the watch face keeps showing the time and offers the unlock.

48-hour refund, no questions asked

As in the Connect IQ Store, we refund the full amount if you ask within 48 hours of your purchase. You don't need to give a reason.

After 48 hours

If the watch face doesn't work as described in its listing, you have the legal guarantee: we fix it or, if that isn't possible, we refund you.

How to ask

Write to contact@amalo.es with the email you used to pay and, if you have it, the watch code shown on the payment page.

We refund to the same payment method (PayPal or card) within 14 days of your request. After the refund, the watch face locks again the next time the watch connects.

Withdrawal in the European Union

The unlock is digital content delivered at once. Before you pay, the payment page asks you to agree that it starts immediately and to acknowledge that you therefore lose the 14-day right of withdrawal. If you were not asked, you keep that right.

Your rights

This policy does not limit your statutory rights as a consumer.
